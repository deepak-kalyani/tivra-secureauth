Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: March 2026

Short version: Tivra SecureAuth stores your TOTP secrets locally in your browser and never transmits any data to external servers. The extension works entirely offline. No personal information is collected, tracked, or shared — ever.

No data collected Works fully offline No external servers No account required

1. Who we are

Tivra SecureAuth is a browser extension developed and published by Deepak Kalyani. This privacy policy explains how the extension handles your data.

2. Data we collect

We collect absolutely no personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information.
  • We do not collect usage statistics, analytics, or telemetry.
  • We do not collect crash reports or diagnostics.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies of any kind.

3. How TOTP secrets are stored

Your TOTP secret keys are stored exclusively using the browser's built-in chrome.storage.local API. This means:

  • Secrets are stored locally on your device only.
  • Secrets are never transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.
  • Secrets are managed by your browser's native storage — the same storage used by other trusted extensions.
  • Uninstalling the extension permanently deletes all stored secrets from your device.

4. Network activity

Tivra SecureAuth makes zero network requests. The extension:

  • Does not contact any external server or API.
  • Does not phone home, check for updates via the network, or send any data outbound.
  • Works fully offline — no internet connection is required to generate TOTP codes.
  • The TOTP algorithm (RFC 6238) is implemented entirely in local JavaScript using your browser's native Web Crypto API.

5. Permissions used

The extension requests only two permissions:

  • storage — to save your TOTP account names and secrets locally in the browser.
  • clipboardWrite — to let you copy a one-time code to your clipboard with one click.

No other permissions are requested. The extension does not access your browsing history, tabs, web requests, or any other browser data.

6. Third-party services

Tivra SecureAuth does not integrate with, communicate with, or share data with any third-party service. No analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or external libraries are included in the extension.

7. Children's privacy

This extension does not target children and does not knowingly collect any information from users under the age of 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be published at the same URL and the "last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the extension after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
